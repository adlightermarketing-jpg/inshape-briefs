IN-SHAPE · CREATIVE STRATEGY

Creator Brief

Four reference formats, each with a video and a script outline. They are direction, not word for word, so make them your own. Shoot vertical, in real settings, on your phone.

CONCEPT 1 OF 4

Read Reviews

VIDEO

WHAT WORKS

It opens with something visually provoking, so the viewer has to know what happens next. Then it sounds like a complaint: you are returning the product, and every reason you give is really a reason to buy it. The real reason, the offer, arrives last, followed by a quick reason to act.

HOW WE ADAPT IT

1. Hook (visual)

Start with something provoking on screen. Hold the tank top with a pair of scissors as if you are about to cut it. You can use a lighter or anything else that creates the same expectation.

Stay safe, and do not actually damage the product. The goal is to make the viewer wait for what comes next.

2. Setup

“This is why you always read the reviews before buying something online. I just picked up the InShape tank top, and I'm going to have to return it.”

3. The fake reasons

“And it's not because it stays in place all day, because that's actually pretty cool. And it's not because it goes under any shirt and nobody can tell you're wearing it. Or because it's breathable and doesn't hold onto smell, because that's actually pretty nice, too.”

4. The real reason

“It's because I just learned they're doing a sale where the more you buy, the more you save. Buy 3, get 3 free.”

5. Call to action

“And it's always better with more tank tops. So go pick some up, because some sizes sell out.”

CONCEPT 2 OF 4

Too Expensive

VIDEO

WHAT WORKS

It opens by saying out loud the objection the viewer is already thinking, so the price stops being a risk. The “Yes, it does this” stack then answers that objection one benefit at a time. The offer arrives last, as the reason to act now.

HOW WE ADAPT IT

1. Hook

“These tank tops are way too expensive.”

2. Introduce the product

“This is the InShape compression tank top.”

3. The “Yes” stack

“Yes, it has real compression. Yes, it stays in place all day. Yes, it's swimwear-grade nylon, so it's light and comfortable. Yes, it's breathable and doesn't hold onto smell. Yes, it goes under any shirt and nobody can tell you're wearing it. Yes, it comes in [mention whatever colors you have].”

If you have a Dad Bod, add: “Yes, it smooths out the belly and the man boobs.”

4. The turn

“But it's too expensive. At least normally it is.”

5. The offer

“Right now it's buy 3, get 3 free, so you're getting six tank tops.”

6. Call to action

“So I recommend getting a pack, but only while the deal is still on. I'll leave the link below.”

CONCEPT 3 OF 4

ASMR Hook

VIDEO

WHAT WORKS

The snap sound at the start grabs attention before anyone has heard a word. The hook then names a question the viewer already has, and the script answers it with the product, one clear benefit, and a quick demo. It closes on the offer.

HOW WE ADAPT IT

1. Hook

“(snap) If you ever wonder how guys wear fitted shirts without lines, bunching, or anything riding up, it's because we're wearing this.”

Pull the shirt away from your body and let it snap back hard enough to make a clear sound, before you say a word.

2. Introduce the product

“The InShape compression tank top. It's seamless-looking under a shirt, and nobody can tell it's there.”

3. Benefits

“It's breathable, which matters because I sweat a lot, and it doesn't hold onto smell. It stays in place all day with no rolling up.”

4. Sizing

“For reference, I'm [height], wearing a [size], and I usually wear a [size].”

5. Offer

“Right now it's buy 3, get 3 free, so you can stock up for every day of the week.”

6. Call to action

“Once you wear one under your shirt, you're not going back. I'll link it below, but some sizes go fast, so grab yours.”

CONCEPT 4 OF 4

No Face

VIDEO

WHAT WORKS

The creator never shows their face, so the viewer focuses on the product, how it fits, and how it looks under clothes. It works for creators who don't want to be on camera, and it still sounds personal because of the sizing line and the offer to help in the comments. Film from the chin down, or only the torso and hands. Voiceover or talking off-camera is fine.

HOW WE ADAPT IT

Script 1: Audience callout

1. Hook

“For the guys that want a clean, put-together look without feeling overdressed, this is the one.”

2. Introduce the product

“This is the InShape compression tank top. It works for work, errands, or a nice dinner, because nobody can tell you're wearing it.”

3. Benefits

“It's light, stretchy, and breathable, and it stays in place all day. So you feel clean and put together but still comfortable.”

4. Show the outfit

“Put it on, tuck it in, throw any shirt over it, and everything just flows.”

Show the shirt going on over the tank.

5. Sizing

“I'm [height], I'm wearing a [size] here, and I usually wear a [size].”

6. Call to action

“Some colors and sizes go fast, so if you see the one you like, I wouldn't wait around.”

Script 2: Colors and value

1. Colors

“I got [mention whatever colors you have].”

2. The offer

“Right now it's buy 3, get 3 free, so you get six tank tops for the price of three.”

3. Features

“Swimwear-grade nylon, breathable, doesn't hold onto smell, and it stays in place under any shirt.”

4. Sizing

“For size reference, I'm [height] wearing a [size]. If you need help with sizing, just let me know in the comments and I got you.”

5. Call to action

“A lot of sizes and colors go fast with this deal, so if you want to see if yours is still available, I left the link below.”
